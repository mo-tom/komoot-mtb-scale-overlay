The slope overlay is independent of MTB Scale. Plan or edit a route after loading the extension so it can capture Komoot’s routing response.

Difficulty grades

Solid line colors are customizable. Use the checkboxes in the map legend to show or hide grades. Grade matching keeps variants such as “S2 (↑S3)” under their main grade, S2.
